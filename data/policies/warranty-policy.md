# Kaveri Home Store - Warranty Policy (fictional sample data)

Effective: 1 April 2026

1. Kitchen appliances carry a 1-year manufacturer warranty from the date of delivery.
2. Furniture carries a 2-year warranty against manufacturing defects (not against wear and tear).
3. Mattresses carry a 5-year warranty against sagging deeper than 2.5 cm.
4. To claim warranty, customers must share the order ID and photos through the support portal.
5. Warranty is void if the product was repaired by an unauthorised service centre.
6. Gold-tier members get an extended 6-month warranty on all appliances.
