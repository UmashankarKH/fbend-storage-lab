# Kaveri Home Store - Refund Policy (fictional sample data)

Effective: 1 April 2026

1. Customers can request a full refund within 30 days of delivery for unused items in original packaging.
2. Opened electronics can be refunded within 10 days if the product is defective.
3. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days after the item is received at our warehouse.
4. Gold-tier members get free return pickup. Other members pay a Rs. 99 pickup fee.
5. Clearance and "final sale" items cannot be refunded, only exchanged for store credit.
6. Damaged-in-transit items must be reported within 48 hours of delivery with photos.
